# Worknoon Refund Policy

Version: 1.0

## Eligibility

1. Final sale items are not eligible for refunds.
2. Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for automatic refunds.
3. Refund requests above $500 require human review.
4. Damaged or incorrect items may qualify for approval.
5. Suspicious or conflicting requests must be escalated.

## Decision Rules

-   APPROVED:
    Request satisfies all automatic refund requirements.

-   DENIED:
    Request violates a non-reviewable refund policy rule.

-   ESCALATED:
    Request requires human review due to amount, suspicious behavior,
    conflicting information, or another review condition.
